# -𝗔𝗺𝗮𝘇𝗼𝗻 𝗣𝗿𝗶𝗺𝗲 𝗩𝗶𝗱𝗲𝗼 𝗗𝗮𝘀𝗵𝗯𝗼𝗮𝗿𝗱 
In this project, I analyzed 𝗔𝗺𝗮𝘇𝗼𝗻 𝗣𝗿𝗶𝗺𝗲 𝗩𝗶𝗱𝗲𝗼 content data to uncover valuable insights related to:

✅ Movies & TV Shows Distribution
✅ Content Growth Over the Years
✅ Genre Popularity Analysis
✅ Ratings Classification
✅ Country-wise Content Distribution
✅ Entertainment Trends & Insights

🔑 KPI Metrics Used
- Total Titles: 9655
- Total Ratings: 25
- Total Genres: 519
- Total Directors: 5771
- Content Timeline: 1920 – 2021

📈 Key Insights :
✔️ Movies accounted for more than 80% of the total content available on Amazon Prime Video.
 ✔️ The platform experienced rapid content growth after 2015, showing aggressive expansion in digital streaming.
 ✔️ The United States contributed the highest number of shows compared to other countries.
 ✔️ Drama and Comedy emerged as the most popular genres on the platform.
 ✔️ Content rated 13+ had the highest number of titles, indicating strong focus on teen and family audiences.
 ✔️ TV Shows represented a smaller share compared to Movies but showed steady growth over recent years.
 ✔️ The dashboard highlights how Amazon Prime Video expanded globally with diverse genres and multilingual content.
